## When scraping makes sense

If your team manually copies leads from directories, marketplaces, or competitor sites, a Python pipeline pays for itself quickly.

## Pipeline stages

1. **Extract** — requests, Playwright, or Scrapy
2. **Clean** — normalize phones, emails, company names
3. **Deduplicate** — hash-based or fuzzy matching
4. **Export** — CSV, Google Sheets, or CRM webhook

## Reliability tips

- Respect rate limits and robots.txt
- Retry with exponential backoff
- Log failures for debugging
- Schedule with cron or n8n
