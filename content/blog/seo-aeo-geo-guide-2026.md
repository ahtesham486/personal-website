## SEO — Search Engine Optimization

Classic Google ranking: meta tags, sitemap, fast pages, backlinks, and useful content.

## AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

Optimizing for AI overviews and tools like Perplexity and ChatGPT search:

- FAQ schema markup
- Clear question-and-answer content
- Consistent entity info (name, services, location)

## GEO — Generative Engine Optimization

Getting cited by generative AI systems:

- Authoritative blog posts
- Case studies with real outcomes
- Mentions on LinkedIn, GitHub, directories

## Technical checklist

- Separate URLs for blog and projects
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`
- JSON-LD Person + FAQ + BlogPosting schema
- Server-rendered or static HTML (Next.js helps)

## Build your brand long-term

One portfolio page is not enough. Publish regularly, interlink posts, and keep your expertise visible across the web.
